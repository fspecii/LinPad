# Built-in wallpapers

dunes.jpg, peaks.jpg, nebula.jpg and waves.jpg were generated for iSH's desktop with a
short Python/Pillow script (layered gradients, curves and blur; no third-party imagery).
They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

meadow.jpg, aurora.jpg, aurora-night.jpg, platinum.jpg, aqua.jpg, sonora.jpg, berry.jpg,
dots-light.jpg and dots-dark.jpg are the desktop themes' wallpapers, generated by
themes/desktop-themes/make-wallpapers.py (NumPy/Pillow: gradients, curves, noise, blur; no
third-party imagery). Also CC0 1.0.
